About Nouriva

Thoughtfully prepared meals made to bring the comfort of home into your everyday routine.

OUR BEGINNING

It Started With A Simple Meal.

Nouriva was created around a simple idea: everyday meals should feel as comforting as something made at home.

Modern routines often leave little time for planning, shopping and preparing every meal from scratch. We wanted to make that part easier without taking away the feeling of a thoughtfully prepared meal.

So we built Nouriva around fresh home-style cooking, flexible meal plans and convenient delivery — giving people a simpler way to enjoy good food throughout their week.

THE NOURIVA PHILOSOPHY Cook thoughtfully. Eat comfortably.
